# Product vision

Three connected layers: AI Workforce supports operations; AI Management converts executive requests into accountable work; AI Transparency exposes the evidence and assumptions behind business figures. The first audience is Russian-speaking SMBs in Russia/CIS, with English engineering material for potential international hardware partners.

The value is useful work with bounded authority, not an ungoverned chat agent. Transparency concerns business events and source quality, not hidden surveillance, personality judgments or opaque employee rankings. Locality supports prepared models and existing data while connectivity is unavailable; it cannot obtain unavailable remote records or send messages without a connector.

Industry packages may target construction, service companies, retail, production, logistics and clinic administration. Medical diagnosis and prescribing are outside scope. P1/P2 contracts and readiness gates are in ROADMAP.md.
