# Customer demonstration walkthrough (8–10 minutes)

Prepare with `make demo`, then privately obtain a login token using `make credential`. Open http://127.0.0.1:8090. Initial image/package downloads require network; the prepared demo needs no paid API, GPU, Ollama or target AI station. Use `synthetic-request.docx`, `synthetic-terms.pdf` and `synthetic-catalog.csv` from `examples/customer-demo/`, or non-confidential files chosen for the demonstration. Do not treat this as production provisioning.

1. **Explain the data boundary.** Show the alpha version and deterministic demo engine. Northline company/finance are synthetic. An uploaded customer file remains user data; it does not become a connected accounting source. Switch EN/RU as needed.
2. **Load a source document.** In Knowledge, upload the sample request PDF or DOCX. Open its extracted source, page/paragraph/table-row anchor and original download. Ask a question about the imported text and follow a citation. The demo returns literal permitted snippets, using hash/lexical retrieval; this is not a measured semantic-quality result. A scan without a text layer should visibly require OCR.
3. **Load a price catalog.** In Commercial proposals, download the template or import the supplied CSV/XLSX. Show the catalog version, item rows, RUB currency and prices excluding VAT. The import rejects formulas and unknown currency; it does not evaluate Excel formulas.
4. **Prepare and check a quote.** Select the source request/version and catalog. Request suggested lines, then check every SKU and quantity. Demo recognizes only explicit `STEEL-01 × 5` notation and always asks for review. Unknown items cannot be priced. Adjust quantity/discount deliberately, save and open Why to inspect the exact Decimal calculation and catalog source rows.
5. **Export and approve.** Download the unapproved quote as DOCX/PDF and show the DRAFT label. Choose a same-team active assignee, a deadline and explicit acceptance criteria. Submit the quote for approval and inspect its version/hash. Before approval, no related task exists. Approve as the creator and wait for worker completion; download the approved version. No email has been sent.
6. **Control the task.** Open Tasks and use My, Today, Overdue or Blocked filters. Choose a near/past deadline in this synthetic example to demonstrate the relevant filter. Change a task to In progress or Blocked. The on-demand briefing shows complete counters and links to stored task records; it does not schedule notifications. Completed tasks are excluded from overdue counts.
7. **Show revision safety.** Change the quantity or assignee in a quote awaiting approval. Its new version requires new approval, and the old queued action cannot execute. Repeated approval does not create duplicate tasks. Revising a quote already executed leaves its earlier task as historical evidence.
8. **Verify roles and audit.** An employee login (`make credential ROLE=employee`) sees permitted team tasks and documents, can update their own assigned tasks, and cannot read management quotes/metrics or another author's workflow. Return to the owner and show audit events plus System capability/validation labels. The hardware target remains “Target hardware; not yet validated on device.”

The earlier Chief of Staff example remains available: request three steel offers, confirm team/deadline/optional assignee, prepare and approve the plan, and update its three persisted tasks. Business Control still shows synthetic forecast margin 20% with contract/cost source rows; quote totals are not booked revenue.
